## About

I design and build autonomous robotic systems end to end, from mechanical structure and embedded firmware through to ROS2 navigation and AI based perception. I'm the founder of **Beam Robotics**, where every project I build is developed as a real product rather than a standalone exercise, each one registered on a centralized platform for monitoring and control.

I hold a B.Eng in Mechanical Engineering from Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia (UTHM) and am a **Certified SolidWorks Associate (CSWA)**. I'm currently pursuing a Master's in Robotics or Autonomous Systems, with a focus on multi-robot coordination and field deployed autonomy.

## Beam Robotics

Beam Robotics is an applied robotics initiative I founded to take autonomous systems from prototype to real deployment. Every project below is built as a product under this umbrella, registered on a centralized cloud platform that gives remote monitoring, control, and telemetry across the full fleet, whether that unit is a security robot, an access gate, or a field robot.

---

## Featured Projects

### [Beam AgroBot V2](https://github.com/xaatim/beam_agrobot_v2)
**Fully autonomous mobile manipulator for precision agriculture**

A ROS2 based robot that combines SLAM navigation, a 5-DOF robotic arm, and an RGB-D vision pipeline to detect and water crops without human intervention. Navigation runs through Nav2 and AMCL against a saved map, while arm control uses MoveIt2 with TRAC-IK kinematics, chosen for its reliability near joint limits, planning collision free trajectories with OMPL. The vision pipeline localizes crops in 3D space and hands targets directly to the arm, closing the full navigate, detect, and water loop autonomously. Currently being extended with autonomous charging, state of charge triggered docking, and multi robot fleet coordination.

`ROS2 Humble` · `Nav2` · `MoveIt2` · `TRAC-IK` · `OMPL` · `RealSense D435`

---

### [Beam Command Center](https://github.com/xaatim/Beam-Command-Center)
**Centralized SaaS platform for the entire Beam Robotics fleet**

Every hardware unit registers to this platform through a cryptographic serial key, giving multi-tenant authentication, role based access control, and real time bi-directional telemetry across all deployed products. Built to scale across product lines rather than a single robot.

`Next.js 15` · `PostgreSQL` · `Drizzle ORM` · `Socket.io`

---

### [Autonomous Surveillance Robot](https://github.com/xaatim/Autonomous_security_robot)
**Self navigating security robot with biometric identification**

Built on the SCUTTLE-Intel AMR platform in collaboration with Katapult Asia (Malaysia). Combines SLAM based indoor navigation with a facial recognition engine for real time identification, and a store-first forensic protocol that secures evidence locally before cloud transmission, supporting autonomous patrol with real time alerts.

`ROS2 Humble` · `SLAM Toolbox` · `InsightFace` · `LiDAR` · `Docker`

---

### [Smart Access Control System](https://github.com/xaatim/SmartAccessControl)
**Industry 4.0 access control and surveillance system**

Integrates facial recognition and automatic license plate recognition for restricted area and parking management. Distributed ESP32 and Arduino edge nodes control boom gates, solenoid locks, and attendance logging, with a handheld ESP-NOW override unit supporting two-way voice communication for on-site security response.

`Python` · `InsightFace` · `EasyOCR` · `ESP32` · `Arduino`

---

### [Beam Agri-Bot V1](https://github.com/xaatim/Smart-Agricultural-Robot)
**Field-tested precursor to AgroBot V2**

A teleoperated precision agriculture platform that validated the core detection-to-actuation loop before AgroBot V2's autonomy upgrade. Uses YOLO-World zero-shot detection to identify crops with no localized retraining, triggering targeted pump actuation once a plant is confirmed within the dosing zone.

`YOLO-World` · `OpenCV` · `Arduino` · `Raspberry Pi`

---

### Smart Water Control System
**Automated water distribution and billing for water purification companies in Somalia**

Combines fingerprint and RFID based authentication with automated flow control and tamper resistant usage logging, running live in an infrastructure-constrained environment.

`Industrial IoT` · `Fingerprint Biometrics` · `RFID`

---

### [Glove Integrity Testing System (GITS)](https://github.com/xaatim/Glove-Integrity-Tester)
**Automated leak detection for medical and laboratory gloves**

A pressure decay based leak detector built for manufacturing quality control. Single-button operation handles pressurizing, monitoring, and evaluating against configurable pass/fail thresholds, with an auto-deflation safety mechanism to prevent over-pressurization.

`Arduino` · `MPS20N0040D` · `HX710B` · `L298N`
